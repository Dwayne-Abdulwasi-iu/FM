# OWNERSHIP Inspection Period Guide for Buyers
## Your 7-Day Preview Before Full Ownership

---

## 🎯 What is the Inspection Period?

When you purchase **exclusive OWNERSHIP** of a design, you get a **7-day inspection period** to review your purchase. During this time:

- ✅ You can view a **preview** of the design
- ✅ You can request clarifications from the seller
- ✅ You can cancel and get a full refund (no questions asked)
- ❌ You CANNOT download the full design files yet
- ❌ You CANNOT use the design commercially yet

**After 7 days**: If you don't cancel, full files unlock automatically and you own the design forever.

## 📹 Understanding the Preview File

### What You'll See

The seller provides a **preview file** (video or image) that shows:
- What the design looks like
- Design quality and details
- How to use the design
- Color options and variations

### Preview Types

**📹 Video Preview** (Recommended)
- Duration: 30 seconds to 2 minutes
- Resolution: 240p (reduced quality)
- Content: Seller explaining the design
- You can: Watch, pause, replay
- You cannot: Download, save, or screen record

**🖼️ Image Preview** (Alternative)
- Screenshot of the design
- Resolution: Up to 800px wide
- Quality: Compressed for preview only
- You can: View, zoom, inspect details
- You cannot: Download or save in full quality

### Example Preview Information

```
PREVIEW FILE

📹 Video: 60-second design walkthrough
Description: "Hand-drawn vintage fashion illustration with 
detailed color variations. Perfect for print-on-demand, 
t-shirt design, or poster."

⏱️ Inspection Period: 5 days remaining
```

## ✅ How to Inspect the Design

### Step 1: Review the Preview
1. Go to your **Purchases** → **OWNERSHIP**
2. Click **"View Preview"**
3. Watch video or view image carefully
4. Take notes of questions

### Step 2: Read the Ownership Agreement
1. Click **"View Ownership Agreement"**
2. Review all legal terms
3. Confirm what you're buying:
   - 100% exclusive ownership
   - All intellectual property rights
   - Commercial use allowed
   - Modification rights included
   - Seller retains no rights

### Step 3: Check Details
```
Confirm:
□ Design matches description
□ Quality meets expectations
□ File formats listed are available
□ Color options are complete
□ No hidden conditions
```

## 🚩 Warning Signs to Watch For

### RED FLAGS
- ❌ Preview doesn't match description
- ❌ Design quality is lower than expected
- ❌ Seller unresponsive to questions
- ❌ Missing features mentioned in listing
- ❌ Preview video is unclear or professional

### WHAT TO DO
If something seems wrong:
1. **Don't cancel immediately** - Ask seller for clarification
2. **Request additional preview** - Ask for more angles/details
3. **Give them 24 hours** - Most sellers respond quickly
4. **If unsatisfied**: Cancel within 7 days for full refund
5. **Contact support** - Report if seller is unresponsive

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why can't I download full files during inspection?**
A: Protection for both parties. Seller prevents theft, you prevent overpaying for design you don't want.

**Q: What if preview doesn't show full design?**
A: Contact seller to request additional preview or clarification. You can always cancel within 7 days.

**Q: Can I screen record the preview video?**
A: The system prevents automatic recording. If you need more details, ask seller for clarification.

**Q: What if I cancel after 7 days by mistake?**
A: Once 7 days expire, ownership is permanent. Contact support, but refunds after that date aren't possible.

**Q: Can I modify the design after I own it?**
A: Yes! You own it completely. Modify however you want. Seller has no say.

**Q: What if seller promises something not in the contract?**
A: Only what's in the ownership agreement matters legally. Get everything in writing.

**Q: Can I re-sell the design?**
A: Yes! You own it. You can sell, license, or do whatever you want with it.

**Q: How do I contact the seller?**
A: Use the platform's messaging system. Response time usually 24 hours.

**Q: What if files are corrupted after download?**
A: Contact seller immediately. They're responsible for file integrity.

**Q: Is there a guarantee on design quality?**
A: Your protection is the 7-day inspection period. If unsatisfied, cancel.
