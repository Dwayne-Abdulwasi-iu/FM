# OWNERSHIP Preview File Guide for Sellers
## How to Protect Your Design While Allowing Buyer Inspection

---

## 🎯 What is the Preview System?

When you sell exclusive **OWNERSHIP** of a design, the buyer gets a **7-day inspection period** to review what they're purchasing. However, you don't want them to get full files and then cancel the purchase while keeping your design.

**Solution**: Upload a **preview file** that:
- Shows the buyer what the design looks like
- Prevents them from downloading/using the full files during escrow
- Gets automatically locked if they cancel
- Unlocks forever after 7 days (if they keep the purchase)

---

## ✅ How It Works

### Timeline for Buyer

| Day | What Happens |
|-----|-------------|
| **0** | Buyer purchases OWNERSHIP option for your design |
| **0** | Buyer sees your **preview file** (video or image) |
| **1-7** | Buyer can watch/view preview to inspect design quality |
| **1-7** | Buyer **CANNOT** download full design files |
| **1-7** | Buyer can cancel anytime → Refund issued → Preview locked forever |
| **8+** | If buyer didn't cancel → Full design files automatically unlocked |
| **8+** | Buyer now owns the design forever, can use/modify/sell |

### What Buyer Sees During Escrow (Days 1-7)

```
Preview Information:
├─ File Type: Video (recommended) or Image
├─ Resolution: 240p (video) or 800px wide (image)
├─ Your Description: "This is a hand-drawn vintage fashion illustration..."
└─ Duration: Full preview to inspect quality
```

---

## 📹 Recommended Preview Format: VIDEO

**Why video is best**:
- You can explain the design
- Show details and usage
- Demonstrate complexity
- Build confidence in buyer
- 30 seconds to 2 minutes ideal

**Video Guidelines**:
- **Resolution**: 240p or lower (auto-compressed)
- **Format**: MP4, WebM, or MOV
- **Size**: Up to 50MB
- **Content**: Explain design, show details, mention usage rights
- **Audio**: Optional but recommended

**Example Script** (60 seconds):
```
"Hi! This is [Design Name]. I created this [hand-drawn/digital/etc] illustration
showing [description]. 

The design includes [key features]. Perfect for [use cases].

You can modify, sell, license, or use this design however you want.
All files - PSD, PNG, vectors - are included.

Questions? Feel free to reach out. Enjoy your purchase!"
```

---

## 🖼️ Alternative: Static Image Preview

If you prefer an image:
- **Best for**: Showing the final design result
- **Resolution**: Reduced automatically (800px max width)
- **Format**: JPG, PNG, WebP
- **Size**: Up to 50MB
- **Content**: Screenshot of finished design showing quality and details

## 📋 Step-by-Step: Upload Preview

### Step 1: Navigate to Design Upload
1. Go to **Upload Design**
2. Choose **OWNERSHIP** option (or **BOTH** which includes OWNERSHIP)
3. Review the ownership document terms
4. Click **"Add Preview File"**

### Step 2: Select Preview File
```
Choose File:
[Select Video or Image File] ← Click to browse

✓ Video (MP4, WebM, MOV)  - Recommended for detailed explanation
✓ Image (JPG, PNG, WebP)  - Good for quick preview

⚠️ Maximum file size: 50MB
```

### Step 3: Add Description (Optional)
```
Preview Description:
[Explain what buyer will see]

Examples:
- "60-second video walkthrough of design"
- "Showcase image with all design elements visible"
- "Before/after demonstration of design application"
```

### Step 4: Submit
1. Click **"Upload Preview"**
2. System compresses:
   - Video → 240p resolution (low quality)
   - Image → 800px max width (compressed)
3. Verification: "Preview uploaded successfully ✓"

## 🔍 What Happens During Buyer's Inspection

### Days 1-7: Escrow Period

Buyer clicks **"Download Design"**:
```
RESPONSE: Preview File Available

📹 Preview Video (240p)
[Play Preview Button]

Description: "60-second design explanation"

---

⏰ Inspection Period: 6 days remaining

⚠️ Full design files will be available after inspection period.

[CANCEL PURCHASE - Get Refund]
```

**Key Point**: Buyer CAN'T get full files. Only preview.

### If Buyer Cancels (Days 1-7)

1. Buyer clicks **"Cancel Purchase"**
2. Refund issued automatically
3. Preview file **immediately locked**
4. Buyer can NEVER access full files
5. Your design is safe ✅

### If Buyer Waits (Day 8+)

1. Inspection period automatically expires
2. Full design files **automatically unlock**
3. Buyer can now download everything:
   - PSD/Source files
   - PNG/Image files
   - Vector files
   - All variations
4. Ownership is now **permanent and irreversible**
5. You receive final payment

## 🚀 Best Practices for Preview Files

### DO ✅
- **Show quality**: Let previews demonstrate craftsmanship
- **Explain usage**: Tell buyer what they can do with design
- **Highlight features**: Point out what makes design unique
- **Build confidence**: Convince them of fair price
- **Keep it short**: 30 seconds to 2 minutes is ideal

### DON'T ❌
- **Don't show full source files**: Preview only, not final files
- **Don't include watermarks**: Preview should look professional
- **Don't make it confusing**: Clear and straightforward
- **Don't include other designs**: Only this design
- **Don't add misleading info**: Be honest about what's included

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Can I change the preview after a buyer purchases?**
A: No. Once purchased, preview is locked. But you can update for future buyers.

**Q: What if I don't upload a preview?**
A: Buyer can't inspect during escrow. They'll see a message asking you to provide one.

**Q: Can the buyer download the preview file?**
A: No. They can only view/watch it. Full files are locked during escrow.

**Q: What resolution should my video be?**
A: 240p or lower (system auto-compresses). Quality is reduced to prevent file saving.

**Q: Can I include audio in the preview video?**
A: Yes! Highly recommended. Explain the design in your voice.

**Q: How long should the preview be?**
A: 30 seconds to 2 minutes. Long enough to show quality, short enough to stay focused.

**Q: What if I made a mistake in the preview?**
A: Before admin approval, you can delete and re-upload. After approval, contact support.

**Q: Does the preview count as a design file I'm selling?**
A: No. It's only for inspection. Buyer gets your actual design files after escrow.
