## **BUDGET-FRIENDLY TOOLS TO CREATE THIS VIDEO**

### **Option 1: DIY Using Free Tools**
1. **Record Screen**: OBS Studio (free, open-source)
2. **Edit Video**: CapCut (free, very user-friendly)
3. **Add Voiceover**: Use your own voice or free text-to-speech (Google Translate)
4. **Music**: YouTube Audio Library (free, copyright-free)
5. **Graphics**: Canva (free version available)
**Estimated Cost**: $0
**Time**: 3-4 hours

### **Option 2: Budget-Friendly Tool**
1. **Loom** ($12/month): Record + edit screen videos
2. **CapCut**: Free editing
3. **Fiverr**: Voiceover artist ($20-50 for 60 sec)
4. **Epidemic Sound**: $100/month for music
**Estimated Cost**: $50-100
**Time**: 2-3 hours (includes Fiverr wait)

### **Option 3: AI Video Tool** (Fastest)
- **Synthesia.io**: Generate video from script
- **HeyGen**: AI avatar with your voiceover
- **Runway ML**: AI video editing
**Estimated Cost**: $100-300
**Time**: 1-2 hours

### **Option 4: Professional Hire** (Best Quality)
- **Fiverr/Upwork**: Video editor + voiceover
- **Local video creator**: Custom shoot
**Estimated Cost**: $300-1,000
**Time**: 5-7 days
