💡 Next Steps to Finalize Your Logo

  • 1️⃣ Choose Your Concept: Which style resonates most with your brand? (Luxury, Modern, Artistic, Professional, etc.)
  • 2️⃣ Select Color Palette: Do you prefer bold colors, subtle tones, or monochrome for versatility?
  • 3️⃣ Refinement: Work with a designer to fine-tune proportions, spacing, and details
  • 4️⃣ Test Across Formats: Favicon (16x16px), App icon (192x192px), Website header, Print materials
  • 5️⃣ Create Brand Guidelines: Document color codes, sizing rules, clear space, usage examples
